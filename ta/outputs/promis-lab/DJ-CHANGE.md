# DJ Change

Choose Fighter → DJ Change, then Play. Four heroes are available; reset retains the selected fighter.

- WASD / arrows: move. Shift: run. DJ Change moves 14% faster than the base player-speed setting.
- J: quick alternating punches, or collect nearby vinyl.
- K: Rewind Kick. Tap again during a kick to queue the next extension; successive kicks alternate front and higher kicks. One queued kick at a time.
- J + K: turning backfist.
- Double-tap left/right: dash ending in a thrust punch.
- Space: neutral jump; then J/K for an aerial attack.
- L with three vinyl: Tempo Burst. Forward rush with blue afterimages, two 8-damage strikes and a final 26-damage knockdown punch. Damage requires an enemy within the forward attack lane; no damage behind. The rush stops short of standing enemies and arena edges.
- P pause; R reset; T slow motion; . advance frame.
