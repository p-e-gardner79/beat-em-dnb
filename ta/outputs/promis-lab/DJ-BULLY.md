# DJ Bully

Select Fighter → DJ Bully, then Play. Reset keeps your fighter selection. All three heroes remain available.

- J: alternating heavy punches; collect nearby vinyl.
- K: heavy boot kick.
- J + K: two-fist hammer blow with knockdown.
- Double-tap left/right: shoulder charge.
- Space: neutral jump; J/K while airborne: punch/boot kick.
- L after three vinyl: Bass-Slam Drop. Grabs a standing enemy ahead within 145 floor units and the same lane, lifts them, then slams them down with a radial burst. With no enemy in grab range, performs the ground burst alone.

DJ Bully is larger, walks at 86% of the player-speed setting and charges at 85% of dash speed. Heavy strikes trade slower recovery for extra damage and knockback. Special costs the full meter, deals 50 base damage per enemy once, and reaches the configured blast radius. The record shop, endless enemies, 1.30× standard test speed, sound effects and Steel Grate Ambush music remain in place.
